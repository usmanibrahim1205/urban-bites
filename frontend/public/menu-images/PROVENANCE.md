# Menu imagery

The local JPEGs were created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for the fictional Urban Bites demo. The menu images are crops of one generated contact sheet, and the banner is a resized copy of a second generated image. No third-party stock photography or remote image URLs were used. The illustrations depict serving suggestions for the demo.

## Menu contact sheet prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Make a single landscape 5 columns by 3 rows contact sheet for an Urban Bites restaurant menu, exactly 15 edge-to-edge equal rectangular photographs, no padding, no dividers, no text, no lettering, no logos. Each cell has a centered single dish with a safe margin, warm cream stone tabletop, muted cream ceramics, natural window light, realistic editorial food photography. Read left to right: row 1 crispy chicken burger with lettuce, double crispy chicken burger with lettuce, flame grilled chicken breast burger with lettuce, beef smash cheeseburger, chicken fajita pizza with bell peppers. Row 2 chicken tikka pizza with red onion, pepperoni pizza, plain golden fries, loaded fries with cheese sauce and spring onion, crispy chicken nuggets and dip. Row 3 tall cola with ice, clear lemon-lime soda with ice, plain unlabeled water bottle beside a glass, chocolate brownie squares, vanilla ice cream sundae with chocolate sauce. Keep every dish within its exact cell, independent composition for cropping.

## Hero prompt

Use case photorealistic-natural. Wide editorial restaurant menu banner photograph. Close view of an appetizing crispy fried chicken burger with naturally craggy crust, shredded iceberg lettuce, sesame brioche, paper-lined ceramic plate and golden fries alongside, casual urban bistro, warm window light and warm caramel shadows, textured sandstone tabletop, cream and burnt-orange hints in the warm scene, sophisticated editorial composition with burger and fries centered in the frame, camera at 25 degrees, photorealistic honest food styling, no text, no people, no packaging, no logos. Horizontal wide crop with subjects in middle vertical third so the image can be cropped to different aspect ratios.
